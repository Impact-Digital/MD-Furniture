About Us

Welcome to MD Furniture House & Contractors, your premier destination for high-quality furniture and professional interior solutions in Shahjahanpur. For years, we have been a trusted name in transforming houses into homes by combining traditional craftsmanship with modern design.
Our Dual Expertise
We take pride in offering a seamless, "one-stop" experience for our clients:
The Showroom (Retail): Located in the heart of Jali Kothi (opposite SBI Bank), our showroom features a curated collection of elegant sofa sets, durable beds, and functional dining furniture. We believe in accessibility, which is why we offer free city delivery to ensure your favorite pieces reach you safely.
The Contractors (Interiors): Beyond retail, our specialized team at M D Interior and Construction handles full-scale residential and commercial projects. From modular kitchens and false ceilings to complete office renovations, we bring architectural precision to every square foot.
